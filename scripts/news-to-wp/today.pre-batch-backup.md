The Sports City concept, part of Noida's Master Plan 2031, required at least 70 per cent of the land to go to sports, institutional and open or recreational use and only about 30 per cent to housing and commercial space. Four large parcels were allotted between 2011 and 2014 across Sectors 78, 79, 150, 151 and 152, the biggest being the roughly 132.9-hectare SC-02 project in Sector 150, allotted to Lotus Greens.
